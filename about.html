About Jennifer Textiles

Embroidery, printing and textile supply in the heart of Kampala.

Jennifer Textiles is based at Nakivubo City Mall in Kampala, Uganda. We supply fabrics, threads, zippers, buttons and other tailoring materials, and we make custom embroidered and printed products for individuals, schools, businesses and tailors.

What we do

  • Textile supply: fabrics, threads, notions and craft supplies, sold by the piece, packet, dozen, carton or roll.
  • Embroidery: logos and names on uniforms, caps, bags and other garments.
  • Embroidery digitizing: turning your artwork into stitch-ready files.
  • DTF printing: full-colour transfers for garments.
  • Screen printing: cost-effective printing for larger batches.

Where we serve

We serve customers in Kampala and across Uganda, and we ship to Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, South Sudan, DR Congo, Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa and more.
