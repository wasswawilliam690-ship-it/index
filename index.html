🎨 Mixing Colors & Buying in Bulk
Every item can be bought as 1pc (single piece), 1pkt (one packet), 1dzn (one dozen = 12 pieces), or 1ctn (one carton, usually 100+ pieces). The Qty box tells us how many of that unit you want — for example, Unit: 1dzn and Qty: 3 means 3 dozens.

You're not limited to one color or one unit per item — add as many combinations as you like, and each shows up as its own line before you send:
Example — ordering Pearl Beads in three different ways at once:
• Black, 1pc, Qty 5  →  5 loose pieces in black
• White, 1dzn, Qty 2  →  2 dozen (24 pieces) in white
• Gold, 1ctn, Qty 1  →  1 full carton in gold
All three lines add into one running total, and one clear itemized order gets sent to us on WhatsApp — no need to place separate orders for different colors or quantities.
