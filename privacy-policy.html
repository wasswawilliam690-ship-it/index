Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Jennifer Textiles ("we", "us") operates this website from Nakivubo City Mall, Kampala, Uganda. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it, in line with the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 of Uganda.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us when you contact us or place an order: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, message, and any photos, files or voice messages you send.
  • Order details such as the items you choose, quantities and order status.
  • Basic technical data your browser shares with websites we use, such as your IP address.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and prepare, produce and deliver your orders.
  • To send order updates, usually by WhatsApp.
  • To keep records of orders and improve our service.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

This website uses services from other providers, including Google Maps and Google Fonts, WhatsApp for messaging, and cloud storage for order records. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Sharing your information

We share your details only when needed to fulfil your order (for example with a courier), or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order and contact records only as long as needed for our business and legal purposes.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us on +256 709 839 014 or WhatsApp.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
