Shipping & Returns

How we deliver your order and what to do if something is wrong.

Where we deliver

We ship from Kampala across Uganda and to Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, South Sudan, DR Congo, Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa and other African countries.

Dispatch and delivery times

Orders are dispatched within 2 to 5 business days depending on the item and destination. Delivery times vary by location. We work with trusted courier partners and send order updates by WhatsApp.

Shipping costs

Shipping rates depend on the destination, weight and courier. We will confirm the cost with you before you pay.

Returns and replacements

  • Custom-made or personalized items (embroidered, printed or digitized to your design) cannot be returned once production begins. Please confirm all details before paying.
  • Damaged or incorrect items are eligible for replacement within 48 hours of delivery. Contact us on WhatsApp with photos of the issue.
