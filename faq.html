Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about orders, delivery and payment.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Most orders (embroidery, DTF, screen printing) take 2 to 5 business days depending on quantity and design complexity. Bulk orders may take longer, and we will confirm a timeline when you place your order.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Most textile supplies have no minimum. Custom embroidery or printing is more cost-effective in bulk, but we accept single-piece orders too.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We deliver within Kampala and across Uganda, and to Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, South Sudan, DR Congo, Nigeria, Ghana and South Africa.

How do I pay?

We accept MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, bank transfer, and cash on delivery or pickup.

Can I get a sample or proof before a bulk order?

Yes. For embroidery digitizing and large print runs we can share a digital proof or a single sample piece first. Mention this when you message us on WhatsApp.

Do you offer refunds or exchanges?

Custom-made items are non-returnable once production starts. Damaged or incorrect items can be replaced if you contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the problem.

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp or visit the contact page.
